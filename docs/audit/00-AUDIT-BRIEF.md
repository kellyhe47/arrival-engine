# Audit brief — Arena Hall Arrival Engine

We are specifying a system that, when a member walks into a private club, emits a
90-second host-facing digest: who arrived, who else present they should meet (with an
exposed score + reasoning), and a short dossier containing at least one fact that is
NOT on the first page of a search result, plus one thing a host could say out loud.

The ten stand-in figures:
Fred Wilson (USV), Brad Feld (Foundry/Techstars), Josh Kopelman (First Round),
Sarah Tavel (Benchmark), Hunter Walk (Homebrew), Steve Huffman (Reddit),
Emmett Shear (Twitch), Eric Ries (Lean Startup/LTSE), Nabeel Qureshi (writer/researcher),
Melanie Perkins (Canva).

We are in the AUDIT phase. We measure ground truth; we do not design.
Rule: a search-result snippet is NOT a source. Open the primary artifact and quote it.
Every claim you record must carry a URL you actually fetched and a verbatim quote.

# Primary-source audit: six expansion candidates

Checked 2026-09-03. This note records the provenance decisions behind ingest prompts 11–16. It is
not a claim that every listed SESSION source was readable without authentication on every attempt.

## Selection standard

- **Identity anchor:** an issuer/company leadership page, current regulatory filing, or exact social
  identity bound by one of those sources.
- **Attributable speech:** a signed submission or letter, a speaker-labeled transcript, an official
  recording, or a post on a strongly corroborated personal account.
- **Context only:** corporate/host biography and editorial narration. These can establish dated
  roles and appearances but must not be rewritten as the subject's own words.
- **Candidate:** a plausible account or protocol key that still lacks the project's required strong
  identity binding. Discovery is allowed; fact collection is not.

## Implementation decision

The six briefs remain expansion plans because the current schema seed and scoring vocabulary are
calibrated to ten members. They intentionally leave prominence unmeasured and candidate topics
uninserted. This avoids laundering stale follower counts, guessed identities, or unreviewed topics
into what otherwise looks like an executable ingest.
